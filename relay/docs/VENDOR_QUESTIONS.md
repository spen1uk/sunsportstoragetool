# Questions to send (read-only: asking these changes nothing on your accounts)

Nothing here touches Kenect, RingCentral, your number or any setting. They are questions only. Paste the answers into the **Kenect Migration → Cutover checklist → Discovery** items as evidence. Suggested: send from the owner's email or the account contact; you don't need to say you're leaving Kenect (e.g. "we're reviewing our messaging setup and have a few questions").

## To Kenect support
Subject: Questions about our account and data exports (262-269-0081)

1. For our number 262-269-0081, how does Kenect send and receive texts? (Does Kenect provide the messaging service, or does it use a messaging service attached to our existing phone line?)
2. Is our number a landline, VoIP or mobile number as far as Kenect is concerned, and is the texting registered under our business (for example 10DLC / campaign registration)? Who is the registered owner?
3. What data can we export, and in what format? Please list each: contacts, full message history (with dates and direction), attachments/photos, saved templates, and opt-out / unsubscribe records. How far back does history go?
4. Is there a self-service export, or do you export it for us? How long does it take, and is it free?
5. What is our contract term, renewal date, and notice period for changes or cancellation? Is there any early-termination fee?
6. If we ever moved texting for this number to another provider, what is your process, and does texting on the number stop immediately or on a set date? Who would we contact?
7. Do you keep our data after we leave, and for how long? Can we get a final export at that time?

## To RingCentral support (or your account admin)
Subject: Confirming how our business number is configured (262-269-0081)

1. For 262-269-0081, what is it configured for today: voice only, or also messaging? Is any text messaging on this number provided by RingCentral?
2. Is this number a RingCentral-owned number or one that was ported in? Who is the carrier of record and what is the line type?
3. Is there any messaging service on the number provided by another company (a third-party text platform)? If so, how is it connected, and could it be affected by changes on your side?
4. What routing is set for calls: business hours, after-hours and voicemail? Please send us a copy or screenshot of the current call-handling rules so we can record a baseline.
5. If the number's **text** handling were moved to a different provider while **calls stay on RingCentral**, is that supported? What must stay the same on your side for voice to be unaffected?
6. Is there a letter-of-authorization, PIN or account-number process we would need, and who is authorized to approve it?

## What I will NOT do
Contact Kenect or RingCentral for you, log in to either account, or change any setting. These stay with you.
