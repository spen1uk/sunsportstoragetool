## Chat proposals with a Confirm button
- ✅ (tested; verified in headless Chromium with seeded proposals; **not yet exercised with the live model**). When a model reply suggests an action, it appears as a card (Confirm / Dismiss) on the Command Center, the chat dock and the Workspace conversation panel. **The model still cannot act**: a proposal is a database row that does nothing until you click Confirm; each one runs once, is re-validated at click time, expires after 24 h, and max 10 can be pending.
- Allow-list only (internal, reversible): add/complete task, queue research (new websites still need their own approval), remember (secrets refused), add/check habit, add person / note contact, add/log learning goal, add quick link. Anything else, or any object with extra fields, is dropped. No sending, spending, deleting, publishing, trading, settings, permissions or approvals.
- The card's wording is built by the server from the validated fields, not from the model's text. The ACTIONS line never reaches the chat history. **Private mode**: the model isn't told about proposals and any it returns are dropped.

## Memory suggestions
- ✅ (tested with a stand-in model; not yet run against the live model). ## Standing permissions
- ✅ (tested) Approvals page → Standing permissions. You can pre-approve **one of four narrow, undoable kinds** of chat suggestion so it runs without the Confirm click: add a task, check off a habit, log learning progress, note you were in touch with someone. Nothing that sends, spends, deletes, fetches a website, saves a memory, completes a task or adds a link can ever be granted (the server rejects them).
- Each grant needs your passphrase/passkey (spent by that one grant), has a **daily cap** (1–30), a **time limit** (1–90 days) and a list of **interfaces** (dashboard, phone, car, voice; texts never). Never created by chat, a device or the model; never inferred from a one-time approval. Revoke is instant. A paused Jarvis never auto-runs anything.
- Every use shows in the reply ("Done under your standing permission: …"), is counted before it runs, and is in the activity log as Jarvis acting under that permission.

## Web search ("ask the internet")
- ✅ (tested with a fake provider; **not yet tried against the real service**) Settings → Web search (off by default; turning it on needs your passphrase/passkey). Then start a message with **`search:`** (or "search the web for …", "google …") and Jarvis answers from the internet with its sources. Works from the dashboard, the phone page and the Siri Shortcut (not by text message). Normal questions never touch the internet.
- How it stays safe: the search runs on **Anthropic's servers** (their web search tool); Jarvis's own server never opens the pages, and the page-fetching tool is not used. A search turn is **walled off**: it sees none of your calendar, tasks, memories or earlier chat, can't propose any action, and gets the stronger model first. Max 3 searches per question and a daily cap (default 15); each search is billed ($10 per 1,000) and counted in your budget, device limits and the daily model ceiling. Answers are shown as "from the internet (unverified)" with plain https sources, are stored flagged, and later chats see them only as a clipped, labelled note.
- Needs: web search must not be disabled for your Anthropic organisation (Claude Console → settings → capabilities). If it is, Jarvis says so.
- Not covered by design: opening a specific logged-in website, filling forms, buying, or reading arbitrary pages itself. A "read this page" feature would be a separate, approval-gated step.

## Master expansion, Phase 2: research router, source map and evidence (approved 2026-10-18)
- ✅ (tested with a scripted provider; ⚪ **not yet run against the real search service**) **Prometheus** (the research agent; the old "Research" node is renamed) with a **Prometheus page** (`#/research`) and a chat shortcut `deep research: <question>`. A **deep** job (budget $2 by default): plans 2–4 angles → one walled-off web-search turn per angle (≤3 searches each) → an extra pass limited to your **trusted** catalog sites when any match → every cited claim saved as an **evidence row** (site, the source's own wording ≤220 chars, page age, time retrieved) → a cross-check model call that sees only the claims (labelled untrusted, page text can't break out of its fence) → report with findings, **contradictions**, gaps, an evidence table and sources. A **quick** job (default $0.10) is one search, not cross-checked.
- **Confidence is computed by us**: a finding is "corroborated by N independent sites" only when its supporting claims come from 2+ different sites; the model's own confidence is ignored; findings that point at no real evidence are dropped; links are stripped; sources older than 18 months are flagged. "Corroborated" does not mean verified true, and the report says so.
- **Resumable and capped**: every finished step is saved with its evidence; a transient failure or restart resumes instead of repeating. The job has its own spending cap and ends with a clearly marked **partial** draft if it reaches it; the monthly cap, daily ceiling and daily search limit still apply. Jobs can never propose actions or see private data. Drafts land in Ready for Review.
- ✅ **Source catalog**: sites with class, status (suggested / trusted / watch / blocked), **your** reliability score, topics, review cadence, terms note. Sites found in research appear as *suggested* (never trusted automatically). Trusted = focused search only; **blocked sites are excluded**; letting Jarvis *fetch* pages is still the separate "Allow fetching…" approval. "Due for review" is shown; automatic re-checking comes with proactive monitoring (later phase).
- ✅ **Politeness for Jarvis's own page fetches** (approved-URL research): honours **robots.txt** (RFC 9309 rules; unreadable robots = skip) and waits 1.5 s between requests to the same site.
- ✅ Legal note: reports on patents/contracts/licences/etc. carry "research only, not legal advice"; on/off switch on the Prometheus page (default on).
- ⚪ Not built (by design or later): free public data APIs (patents, papers, filings) need your approval and a terms review each; proactive/self-started research and monitoring (Phase 4); the router currently chooses between web search, your trusted sites, and the approved-URL path.

## Master expansion, Phase 4: missions, Prometheus and temporary agents (approved 2026-10-18)
- ✅ (tested end to end with a scripted model; ⚪ not yet run against the real search service) **Missions page** (`#/missions`, chat: `mission: your goal`): you assign a goal and a cap ($0.05–$10, default $3); Prometheus **plans 2–5 steps**, runs each as a research job owned by a **temporary specialist agent**, then **Jarvis reviews** the combined result (conclusion, what to verify, honest about thin or partial evidence). Each mission shows status in plain words, priority, owner, why it matters, cost against its cap, next action, blockers, steps with "View results" links, and Jarvis's review; controls: pause, resume, stop, retry, wrap up with what it has, add a step ("ask for more evidence"), dependencies on other missions.
- **Self-started work is deliberately narrow**: only (1) **watches** you create (a topic, how often; plus one-click upgrade-scouting presets for Claude API changes, security advisories for Jarvis's dependencies, and safe-AI techniques) and (2) **follow-up ideas**, which appear as *proposals* that do nothing until you Accept. Self-started missions run at the lowest priority and **yield to anything you assign** (they save progress and resume), at most 2 at a time, 1 new per day, $1 each, **$10 a month in total** (the budget you approved), and stop for the month when it's used. Results reach your morning briefing only if something was backed by several sites; they never push a notification.
- **Temporary agents**: a specialist per step with a charter, its own allowance (≤ $2), at most 3 at once, a hard expiry (default 6 h, max 24 h) and only the read-only skills it was spawned with, each of which Prometheus itself may use (never more than its parent). Removed automatically when the step finishes, the mission ends, it expires or its allowance is used; their skill grants are deleted so the identity stops working immediately; **Stop everything** removes them all and pauses every running mission (they stay paused until you resume).
- **Jarvis ↔ Prometheus**: after a web answer, say **"dig deeper"** and Jarvis hands the question to Prometheus as a thorough research job. Prometheus reports to Jarvis; you hear by your normal contact rules: finished assigned missions send one notification (max 5 a day), stuck ones send one nudge, and nothing repeats.
- ⚪ Not built: missions that propose new *skills*, parallel multi-agent fan-out beyond one step at a time per mission, SMS/call delivery of mission results (no adapter exists; recorded as not sent), and any mission action beyond researching and reporting.
